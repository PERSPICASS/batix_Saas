## ✨ MINDSET & APPROACH

Remember:
- ✅ **Think long-term**: SEO takes 3-6 months to show results
- ✅ **Focus on quality**: 1 great backlink > 10 bad ones
- ✅ **Create for users first**: Google rewards real value
- ✅ **Monitor & optimize**: Data-driven decisions always
- ✅ **Stay consistent**: Regular effort > sporadic bursts
- ❌ **Don't buy backlinks**: Black hat tactics = penalties
- ❌ **Don't over-optimize**: Keyword stuffing = bad for SEO
- ❌ **Don't ignore competitors**: Analyze what they do well
