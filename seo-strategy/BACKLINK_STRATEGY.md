Potential backlinks: 30-50

```

[Your Name]
BATIX PRO
```

**Email Template 2: Follow-up (after 1 week)**

```
Subject: RE: Guest Post Idea for [Blog Name]

Just checking in on my guest post proposal. I'm flexible on:
- Topic (I can pivot based on your current needs)
- Length & format
- Timeline

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2.3 Guest Post Process

#### Step 1: Research (2 hours)
- Lire 5-10 articles du site
- Identifier leur tone, style, audience
- Voir quels sujets performent (commentaires, shares)

#### Step 2: Pitch (1 hour)
- Envoyer email personalisé
- Inclure outline détaillé
- Montrer pourquoi ça fit leur audience

#### Step 3: Écrire (8 hours)
- Suivre leur guidelines exactement
- Format: Headlines, short paragraphs, examples
- Include internal links (2-3 max)
- Include 1 backlink to BATIX PRO

#### Step 4: Édition & Publication (2 hours)
- Répondre aux feedback editor
- Vérifier que backlink est correct
- Promouvoir quand publié

#### Step 5: Follow-up (1 hour)
- Remercier éditeur
- Partager article largement
- Entrer en contact pour future collaboration

## 📢 PILIER 3: PR & Partnerships

### 3.1 Press Release Strategy

**Occasions pour Press Release:**

```
1. Major Feature Launch
   - "BATIX PRO Adds AI Assistant to Business Operations"
   - Distribute via PRWeb, Businesswire

2. Survey Results
   - "Study: 73% of Hardware Stores Still Use Manual Inventory"
   - Très linkable!

3. Partnerships
   - "BATIX PRO Partners with [Supplier/Platform]"

4. Milestones
   - "BATIX PRO Hits 1000+ Active Users"
   - "BATIX PRO Raises Funding / Achieves Profitability"

5. Industry Insights
   - "Hardware Store Trends 2026: What's Changing"
```

**Press Release Distribution:**

```
Tier 1 (Free):
- GlobeNewswire (free tier)
- eReleasesonline
- BusinessWire (free tier)

Tier 2 (Paid, mais high ROI):
- BusinessWire (paid)
- PRWeb (paid)
- GlobeNewswire (paid)

Cost: €50-200 per release
Expect: 30-50 backlinks per release
```

### 3.2 Partnership Backlinks

**Types de Partnerships:**

```
1. Integration Partnerships
   - Partner with accounting software, CRM, payment providers
   - Press release: "BATIX PRO integrates with [Partner]"
   - Backlink: They link to your integration page

2. Co-Marketing
   - Create joint content with complementary company
   - Example: "Guide: Accounting for SMB Retailers"
   - Both promote, both get backlinks

3. Affiliate Programs
   - Create affiliate page linking to referral partners
   - They link to you, you link to them

4. Industry Association Listings
   - Get listed in relevant directories
   - "Directory of Retail Management Software"
   - "List of Quincaillerie Tech Solutions"
```

### 3.3 Media Mentions

**Getting Mentioned in Articles:**

```
Tactic: "Expert Quote" outreach

1. Find relevant articles being written
   - Set Google Alerts: "hardware store software", "SMB tech"
   - Follow journalists covering your space
   - Monitor Muck Rack for opportunities

2. When you find relevant article in draft:
   - Email journalist quickly
   - Offer expert quote or data point
   - "Hey, I saw you're writing about [topic]. 
     Would you like a quote from hardware store expert?"

3. If they include your quote:
   - Usually they link to your website
   - Builds authority & brand
```

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Buy backlinks (black hat, can hurt SEO)
- Link from unrelated sites (low quality)
- Over-optimize anchor text (looks spammy)
- Neglect anchor text variation (natural)
- Ignore backlink quality over quantity

✅ **Do:**
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Vary your anchor text naturally
- Mix brand mentions with keyword mentions
- Monitor competitors' backlinks for opportunities
- Build relationships with webmasters (long-term)
