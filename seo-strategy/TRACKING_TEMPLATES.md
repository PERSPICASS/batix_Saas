## HOW TO USE THESE

**Weekly**: Every Friday, spend 30 min filling out WEEKLY PROGRESS TRACKING
- Gives you momentum awareness
- Identifies what's working
- Helps plan next week

**Monthly**: First day of each month, spend 1 hour on MONTHLY SUMMARY
- Shows trends
- Celebrates wins
- Informs strategy adjustments

**Quarterly**: End of each quarter, spend 2-3 hours on QUARTERLY REVIEW
- Big picture perspective
- ROI analysis
- Strategic planning for next quarter

**Backlink Tracker**: Update as you send pitches and hear back
- Shows your pipeline
- Motivation when results come in
- Portfolio of accomplishments

**Daily (Optional)**: If you want discipline
- Keeps you accountable
- Shows small wins
- Builds momentum
