## 📨 TEMPLATE 1: Cold Guest Post Pitch (High Quality Site)

```
Subject: Guest Post: "Why Hardware Store Software Matters" for [Blog Name]

I've been following [Blog Name] for a while and really appreciated your recent article on [specific article title they wrote].

I'd like to contribute a guest post that I think your audience would find valuable:

**Title:** [Your suggested post title - specific and benefit-driven]
**Length:** 2000-2500 words
**Core Value:** [1-2 sentences on what readers will learn/gain]

Why this fits:
• Your audience includes [SMB owners / retail managers / quincaillerie owners]
• It complements your existing content on [related topic from their site]
• I can include relevant resources that add value (no spam, I promise!)

About me:
I help hardware store owners with [your expertise]. I've published on [other sites you've written for].

I've attached a detailed outline for your review. Happy to adjust based on your guidelines and preferences.

Looking forward to collaborating!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Company]
[Your Website]
[Your Email]
```

## 🎯 HOW TO USE THESE

**Best Practices:**

✅ **DO:**
- Personalize each email (use their name, mention specific article)
- Keep it short (< 150 words ideal)
- Have a clear call-to-action
- Include your credentials briefly
- Make it easy to say yes

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use generic "To whom it may concern"
- Attach your full pitch (link instead)
- Ask for something immediately
- Make it too salesy
- Forget to proofread
